UI based Syntax Tree Generator


Output strings can be pasted into text based tree generators such as https://mshang.ca/syntree/

Currently being user tested. Hover over nodes to interact. Use the red circles to drag nodes. Click '+' to add children at a node, click '-' to remove node.

Mobile usability may be suboptimal.
